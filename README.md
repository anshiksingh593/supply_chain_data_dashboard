# supply_chain_data_dashboard

##📦 Supply Chain Performance & Logistics Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain operations to identify key business insights related to revenue generation, supplier performance, logistics efficiency, product demand, and quality management.

Using Excel, Pivot Tables, Power Query, and Dashboard Visualization techniques, the dataset was cleaned, analyzed, and transformed into an interactive Supply Chain Performance Dashboard that helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

🎯 Business Objective

The goal of this project is to:

Monitor overall supply chain performance.
Identify top-performing products and suppliers.
Analyze revenue contribution across different locations.
Evaluate logistics efficiency through shipping costs and lead times.
Track defect rates to improve product quality.
Support strategic decision-making through data visualization.
🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables
Pivot Charts
Slicers & Interactive Filters
Data Cleaning & Transformation
Dashboard Design

#📈 Dashboard Insights
1. Revenue by Product Type
Skincare generated the highest revenue (~$241K).
Haircare contributed approximately $174K.
Cosmetics generated around $161K.

Insight: Skincare products are the strongest revenue drivers and should receive higher inventory and marketing focus.

2. Revenue by Location

Top-performing locations:

Mumbai (~$138K)
Kolkata (~$137K)
Chennai (~$119K)
Bangalore (~$103K)
Delhi (~$81K)

Insight: Mumbai and Kolkata contribute the largest share of revenue and represent key markets for expansion.

3. Supplier Performance Analysis

Revenue contribution by suppliers:

Supplier 1 → ~$158K
Supplier 2 → ~$125K
Supplier 5 → ~$110K
Supplier 3 → ~$98K
Supplier 4 → ~$86K

Insight: Supplier 1 consistently delivers the highest business value and can be considered a strategic supplier.

4. Defect Rate Analysis
Supplier 5 showed the highest defect rate.
Supplier 1 maintained relatively lower quality issues.

Insight: Suppliers with higher defect rates require stricter quality checks and process improvements.

5. Transportation Mode Analysis
Mode	Avg Shipping Cost	Avg Shipping Time
Air	$6.02	5.12 Days
Road	$5.54	4.72 Days
Rail	$5.47	6.57 Days
Sea	$4.97	7.12 Days

Insight:

Sea transport is the cheapest but slowest.
Road transport provides the best balance between cost and delivery speed.
Air transport is suitable for urgent shipments despite higher costs.

#🔍 Business Recommendations
Revenue Growth
Increase focus on skincare products due to their strong revenue contribution.
Expand marketing efforts in Mumbai and Kolkata markets.
Supplier Management
Strengthen partnerships with Supplier 1 and Supplier 2.
Investigate quality issues associated with Supplier 5.
Logistics Optimization
Use Road transportation for routine deliveries.
Reserve Air transportation for high-priority shipments.
Use Sea transportation for bulk orders where delivery speed is not critical.
Quality Improvement
Implement stricter quality inspections for suppliers with high defect rates.
Monitor defect trends regularly to reduce operational losses.
